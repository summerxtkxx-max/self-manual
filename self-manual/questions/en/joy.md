# Happy moments

How to use: this is ammunition, not a questionnaire. Pick one or two, then unpack the moment into its ingredients (people, place, pace, no pressure, getting something done). ☀️ Easy / 🌤️ Deeper / 🌙 Late-night (only when they're in a good place and up for it; ask first, and they can always skip — see `interviewer.md`). Swap "got home" or "after work" for whatever fits their life.

## ☀️ Easy
- In the last three months, was there an evening you got home and thought, "Today was actually pretty good"? How did that day go?
- Which weekends this past year ended with you thinking, "That was a good one"?
- When did you last laugh really hard? What were you doing, and who were you with?
- Is there a place where you breathe out the moment you arrive?
- What's a tiny thing that reliably puts you in a better mood?
- What made you smile without meaning to recently?
- If you got a whole free afternoon with nothing planned, how would you spend it?
- What's a small joy you've kept for years? (A song, a smell, a corner of the city…)
- In the past week, was there a moment, even just a few seconds, when you thought "this is nice"?
- When did you last laugh out loud alone? What were you looking at?
- Something you ate, heard, or smelled lately that instantly lifted your mood?
- What's your favorite weekend so far this year? Roughly when did you wake up and what did you do?
- Anything small you looked forward to for days lately? How did it feel when it finally came?
- Back in school, what was your favorite part of the day? Why?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- Take your happiest recent moment apart: was it the people, the place, the pace, the lack of pressure, or getting something done?
- If one of those were missing, would it still have felt as good?
- What do your happiest few memories have in common?
- Is there something that felt great to finish even though nobody praised you for it?
- Some days off feel full and others feel wasted. What's the difference?
- When you're happy, do you want to share it right away or savor it alone?
- As a kid, when nobody was telling you what to do, what did you run off to do?
- Ever had a moment of "oh, so that's why I'm happy"?
- Think of your last really good day. If you swapped the people, or swapped the place, which change would ruin it most?
- A planned joy versus a surprise joy from recently: which one stuck with you more, and why?
- Is there something you always expect to enjoy, but when it happens it's kind of flat?
- What gave you way more happiness than you expected in the last year or two?
- School, early working years, now: is there a thread in what makes you happy that hasn't changed?
- Something that didn't feel special while you were doing it, but you miss now?
- Something everyone else seems to love that you secretly don't enjoy?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Is there a kind of joy you feel you don't deserve, or shouldn't be this happy about?
- When were you last so happy it scared you a little? What were you afraid of?
- If you had to tell your future self about your happiness in one sentence, what would it be?
- Is there a joy you've always wanted but never let yourself go after?
- A period that was really happy, but feels a bit bittersweet to remember now? Share only what you want to.
- Recently, a time you were genuinely glad about someone else's good news, and a time you couldn't be?
- A kind of joy you rarely tell people about because it might seem childish or weird?
- If you could keep only one everyday small joy from tomorrow on, which would it be?
- Is there enough joy in your life right now, too little, or have you just not been noticing? Feel free to skip.
