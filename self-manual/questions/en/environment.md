# Comfortable spaces

Lean on concrete scenes, light on psychology. For preferences that matter, ask "if you take it apart, which ingredients make it comfortable?"

## ☀️ Easy
- Of the places you've lived or visited, where did you relax the moment you walked in?
- What kind of light do you like? (Daylight, warm lamps, bright, dim)
- What sound do you want around you? (Total silence, white noise, rain, people chatting, music)
- Home, a café, a library, an office: where do you focus best?
- Any temperatures or smells you love, or can't stand?
- Do you like a space tidy, or a bit lived-in?
- Window seat, corner, or big shared table?
- What should your ideal bedding feel like? (Soft, firm, heavy, light, cool, warm)
- When did you last sit somewhere and just not want to leave? Where was it?
- Which corner of your current home do you spend the most time in? What's there?
- Where do you fall asleep most easily (not necessarily a bed)?
- At school or work, where's your favorite spot to be?
- When booking a place to stay, what's the first thing you look at?
- Is there a kind of weather that makes you want to go outside?
- If you could add one thing to your desk, what would it be?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- Do crowds energize or drain you? What kind of crowd is fine, and what kind isn't?
- Alone in an unfamiliar city versus at home: which feels safer to you?
- What do you like about cities (convenience, buzz, anonymity) and about nature (quiet, space, slowness)?
- Describe your ideal place to live: what floor, what neighborhood, what's nearby?
- Which city have you visited where you thought, "I could live here"? What gave you that feeling?
- Is there a kind of environment that makes you irritable for no clear reason? What's it like?
- What conditions make you really productive at work, and which wear you out fast?
- What's the longest commute you can tolerate, and your favorite way to get around?
- Think of a time you were deeply focused somewhere. What were the light, sound, and people like? Could you do that at home?
- Have you moved? Which move made life noticeably better or worse, and why?
- Open office, private room, home, café: which have you actually used most in the last three months? Is it your favorite?
- What do you need to live close to in order to feel settled? (A market, the subway, a park, friends, water…)
- Is your tolerance for mess the same in every space, like bedroom, desk, kitchen?
- In a new city, how long until it feels like yours? What made it click?
- Where have you felt most like yourself? What made it feel that way?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Is there a place you've wanted to leave for a long time but haven't? What's holding you?
- Forget the building: what does "home" feel like for you?
- Is there anything about your childhood home that still shapes what "comfortable" means to you?
- A place that makes you a little homesick, whether or not it was ever home?
- Have you ever stayed somewhere so long you slowly stopped feeling like yourself? Share only if you want.
- If reality didn't matter at all, where would you live? What's kept you from going?
- Do you have a "safe corner", real or imagined?
