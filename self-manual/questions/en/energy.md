# Energy

Same approach as joy. Talking about drains can pull the mood down, so after one drain, steer back to something that recharges them.

## ☀️ Easy
- What do you finish and think, "Okay, my battery's back"?
- What time of day are you at your best?
- How long alone before you want people? How long with people before you want to be alone?
- What small thing can suddenly sour your mood?
- Do you have a recharge ritual? (A drink, a walk, a shower, tidying up)
- What tends to waste your weekend energy?
- Which day this past week ended with plenty of battery left? What did you do?
- The last time you woke up feeling good, what happened the day before?
- After clearing a pile of messages, do you feel lighter or more drained?
- Has there been a time lately when you really didn't want to go out, but came back with more energy?
- Which small fixed moment in your day lets you breathe? (The commute, after lunch, ten minutes before bed…)
- A few days straight at home, or a few days straight out and about: which drains you faster?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- What bothers you a lot that other people seem to shrug off?
- The last time you suddenly felt wiped out, what happened in the hours before?
- Is there a kind of event you dread beforehand and need ages to recover from?
- What have you been putting off? Is it hard, scary to face, or just pointless-feeling?
- Something you feel you "should" enjoy but actually don't?
- Some busy feels energizing and some feels empty. What's the difference?
- Something you thought would relax you that left you more tired?
- Of the jobs or projects you've had, which drained you most and which least? Why?
- A completely unplanned day: comfortable or empty? When does it tip into empty?
- A recent "tired but happy" and a recent "tired and hollow": what were they, and what's the difference?
- Which conversations cost you most: making decisions, comforting someone, impressing someone, or keeping up appearances?
- What happens to your energy while you're waiting, for a reply, a result, or someone else's decision?
- In the last three months, has something kept draining you that you haven't stopped? What makes it hard to stop?
- When there's no outside structure (a break, a gap, between gigs), how does your energy move?
- Doing something because you chose to versus because you were asked: how is the tiredness different?
- How do you usually notice you're out of battery? Does your body or your mood find out first?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Put together everything you've quit, left, or drifted from. What do they have in common?
- The last time you were pushing through something, what were you pushing through?
- Do you tend to ignore your own tiredness? What makes stopping feel impossible?
- If you could protect only one kind of energy, which would it be?
- Was there a stretch when you were exhausted but wouldn't let yourself stop? Looking back, what were you afraid of?
- Is there a kind of drain you've always thought was "your own fault"? If you want, say when it tends to show up.
- When were you last truly fully charged? How long ago was that?
- If someone really understood your energy, what would you want them to do, and not do, when you're running low?
- Feel free to skip: is there a relationship or situation you know is draining you but can't leave yet?
