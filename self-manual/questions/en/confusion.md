# Stuck points

The area that needs the most care with pacing. Ask how much they want to open up before deciding how deep to go. Drop anything they don't want to touch, without persuading. Keep the tone calm, with few games and emoji. Every question is skippable, and you never ask why. If their mood dips midway, stop and be with them first. Figuring it out today isn't the goal.

## ☀️ Easy
- What's pulling you in two directions lately? One sentence is enough.
- When did it start?
- What's the most tiring part of it right now?
- Today, does it feel more annoying, scary, exhausting, or frustrating?
- Is there one small next step that doesn't require solving the whole thing?
- Is there a question that keeps popping up in the shower, on walks, or before sleep?
- Have you talked to anyone about it? Did you feel better after, or more tangled?
- If your feeling about this were weather, what would it be?
- Was there a moment this week when you forgot about it for a while? What were you doing?
- Is there any part of it you already feel sure about, even a tiny part?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- Between the options, what draws you to each one, and what worries you about each?
- If this were resolved a year from now, how would you hope it got resolved?
- In this tangle, which parts are what you really care about, and which are other people's voices?
- Are you already leaning one way, just not admitting it yet?
- What's the worst outcome you fear? What would you do if it happened?
- When you've been stuck like this before, how did you eventually get out?
- What are you missing right now: information, courage, time, or something else?
- If you didn't have to explain yourself to anyone, which would you choose?
- Think of a decision that turned out well. How did you make up your mind then? Would that method work now?
- Is there information here you've been avoiding finding out?
- If your best friend were in exactly this situation, what would you tell them?
- Is this "I don't know what I want", "I know what I want but I'm scared", or "the things I want are fighting each other"?
- Is there a smaller, cheaper way to try out one of the options first?
- If you gave yourself a "not deciding yet" deadline, how long could you let it wait?
- Does this remind you of a past time you were stuck?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Could the real question underneath this be something else?
- Have you been waiting for "a sign" before acting? What sign are you waiting for?
- If you were a little gentler with yourself, how would you see where you are now?
- Have you been acting like you've already figured it out around other people?
- Take your time, or skip this one: if there's no "right" answer in the end, could you live with that?
- Is it possible you don't really want the original goal anymore?
- If you chose wrong, what scares you more: the consequences, or what people would think?
- What's the harshest thing you say to yourself about this? How would you say it to a friend instead?
- Could it be that what you need right now isn't an answer, but some time, some rest, or someone beside you?
