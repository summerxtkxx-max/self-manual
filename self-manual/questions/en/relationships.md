# Relationships

Only record what they choose to share. Don't press for private details about others. "A friend" or "a coworker" is enough, no names needed. Focus on their own feelings and needs, not on what the other person did privately, and don't judge the other person for them.

## ☀️ Easy
- When did you last feel really at ease with someone? Who were they (roughly), and what were you doing?
- Your favorite way to hang out with friends? (Talking a lot, doing your own things side by side, doing something together, picking up easily after months apart)
- One-on-one, a small group, or a big crowd?
- What do people do that makes you feel well treated?
- Do you reply to messages right away or batch them? Does that feel okay for you?
- Do you prefer giving gifts or receiving them?
- In the past week, did someone say or do something that warmed you up a little? What was it?
- What's the thing you most often do with friends?
- When a get-together ends, are you wishing for more or quietly relieved?
- How do you like to be invited: well in advance, a day or two before, or last minute?
- What's your most relaxed way to talk: in person, voice, text, or chatting while doing something together?
- In school, were you more of a solo person or part of a regular little crew?
- How do you usually react when someone compliments you?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- What kind of people tire you out fast? What do they have in common?
- In which relationships do you feel most like yourself, and least? What's the difference?
- What distance feels right to you? How often in touch, how often seeing each other?
- When you're angry or disappointed, how do you usually handle it, and how do you want others to treat you?
- How hard is it for you to say "I need…"? Which needs are hardest to voice?
- Are you more often the one taking care of others, or being taken care of? Which feels better?
- How do you feel about boundaries? Has anyone crossed one?
- What does a good friend have to be?
- The last time you thought of someone you haven't talked to in ages, what brought them to mind?
- Think of one meetup that left you feeling great and one that left you drained. What did the other person do differently? (No need to say who.)
- After changing schools, cities, or jobs, which relationships stayed? What do you think kept them?
- In a new place, how long and what does it take before you feel you've found "your people"?
- When you disagree with someone, do you say it or let it sit? When do you speak up?
- What kinds of requests do you say yes to even when you don't really want to?
- Among coworkers, classmates, friends, and family, who do you relax most around? Who makes you tense up?
- The last time you reached out to someone first, why?

## 🌙 Late-night
- A relationship you keep maintaining but aren't sure you actually want?
- Have you ever shortchanged yourself because you were afraid of being left?
- What's one thing you wish people understood about you?
- How far apart are what you hope for in close relationships and what you've actually found?
- A relationship that's over but still comes back to you? Which moment usually comes back? No need to talk about the other person.
- Have you been playing a role in a relationship that you don't really want to play?
- What kind of sentence is hardest to say to someone close? (An apology, a no, "I need you"…)
- Where's the biggest gap between how others see you and who you know you are?
- Feel free to skip: is there a kind of loneliness you feel most in a crowd?
