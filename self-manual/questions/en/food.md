# Food

Everyday and sensory. Never about weight, dieting, or body goals. If they bring those up, just listen: no diet advice, and nothing inferred about it in the manual.

## ☀️ Easy
- If you could pick one meal that would make you really happy, what would it be?
- Your three favorite foods, and three you won't touch?
- Are you a proper-breakfast person or a whatever's-around person?
- Is there a little food that always cheers you up?
- Sweet, salty, sour, spicy, bitter: which one do you lean toward?
- Your go-to drink? Hot or cold?
- Any strong feelings about texture? (Crunchy, chewy, slimy, greasy, raw, cold)
- Eating alone, what do you usually go for?
- What's the one bite from this past week you remember most?
- Is there a place you keep going back to? What do you usually order?
- What did you eat all the time in school? Do you still crave it?
- What do you want on a rainy day, in winter, or after a late night?
- Ordering takeout: do you already know what you want, or scroll forever?
- Any little ritual you have with a certain food? (Which part first, what you drink with it)
- Do you like to eat with conversation, with a show on, or in quiet?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- Eating alone versus with a group: which do you prefer, and what pace and mood do you like at the table?
- Do you like cooking? Does it relax you or feel like a chore?
- What do you want to eat when you're down? Is it the same as your everyday favorites?
- Is there a food that reminds you of a particular person or place?
- Eating out, what matters most: taste, atmosphere, price, speed, or who you're with?
- Something you used to love and don't anymore, or the other way around? What changed?
- Would you travel a long way just for one restaurant?
- Familiar flavors or something new: which do you reach for more?
- The meal with someone else you remember most clearly: who, where, and what made it stick?
- Living with family, in a dorm, alone: how did your eating change, and which stretch felt best?
- Who picks the restaurant when you feel most at ease: you, them, or deciding together?
- A flavor you love that the people around you just don't get?
- Going to a restaurant alone: totally at ease, or a bit awkward? Has that changed?
- What makes you decide never to go back to a place?
- When traveling, do you research food spots or eat wherever you end up?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Has eating ever felt stressful or uncomfortable for you? (Only if they want to talk about it.)
- What was the dinner table like growing up? Does it shape how you eat now?
- A dish that instantly makes you think of someone? You can just describe the dish, not the person.
- A taste you really miss but can't get anymore?
- Feel free to skip: right now, is eating more of a pleasure, a way to take care of yourself, or just refueling?
- How far is your ideal "eating with someone" from how you usually eat these days?
