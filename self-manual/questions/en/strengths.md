# Interests and strengths

Same approach as joy. Find strengths in things they've done; never ask them to rate themselves. If they say "I'm not good at anything", don't argue. Switch to a more concrete scene.

## ☀️ Easy
- Anything recently that made you lose track of time?
- What did you love playing on your own as a kid?
- What kind of content could you watch forever without getting bored?
- What makes you stop scrolling?
- When learning something new, do you prefer watching, listening, doing, or talking it through?
- What was the last thing that made you think, "I want to learn that"?
- In the past month, did you finish something and think, "Actually, I'm pretty good at this"?
- What do friends or classmates most often come to you for?
- What was your favorite class or kind of assignment in school? Was it the content, the teacher, or the format?
- Which "making things" app do you use most? (Video editing, notes, drawing, spreadsheets…)
- Is there something you offer to do for others because you'd just be faster?
- What was the last rabbit hole you couldn't stop researching?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- What do people ask you to help with?
- What comes easily to you that you don't think of as a skill?
- What kind of work gives you more energy the longer you do it, and what makes you want to run?
- What have you been praised for that you think is "no big deal"?
- What's the piece of work, project, or result you're proudest of? Which part exactly?
- Is there something you do without anyone pushing you or needing anyone's approval?
- Do you enjoy the process, the result, or how people react afterwards?
- Compared to people your age, what's a little different about you?
- An interest you've thought about turning into a career, but worried you'd stop loving it?
- Think of a project that flowed and one that felt awkward. How did the content, teamwork, or pace differ?
- In a group (school project, work, a club, a trip with friends), what role do you naturally end up in? Is it the one you want?
- A skill that got noticed in one place and ignored in another?
- During gaps, holidays, or unstructured time, what do you pick up on your own?
- What's the latest new thing you learned? Which part of learning it was most fun?
- A piece of feedback you didn't believe at first but later thought might be right?
- When you're stuck, do you grind through, ask someone right away, or step away first? Which works best for you?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Is there a talent you've never quite dared to admit you have?
- Have you ever given up something you loved because of other people's expectations?
- If nobody would judge it, what would you most want to do?
- In what you do now, which part is "I want to" and which part is "I should"?
- Something you're very good at but don't actually enjoy? Why do you keep doing it?
- Is that childhood "when I grow up I want to…" still somewhere in you?
- Has one of your strengths ever cost you something? Which one?
- Something you really want to do but haven't started because you're afraid of doing it badly? You can just name the kind of thing.
