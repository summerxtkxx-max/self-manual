# Values and trade-offs

Same approach as joy. Find values in real choices and emotional reactions, not abstract "what do you value" questions. After any ranking question, always ask for a concrete example.

## ☀️ Easy
- If you could do just one thing today to make it a day well spent, what would it be?
- What can you not stand people doing?
- Do you have a little personal rule? (Like "never lend money" or "no all-nighters")
- What do you admire in someone you like, real or fictional?
- If money weren't an issue, what's one thing you'd never give up?
- The last time you thought, "I did the right thing", what was it?
- A recent news story, post, or real-life moment that made you really angry? What exactly got to you?
- When choosing (shopping, classes, jobs), what do you rule out first?
- The last thing you were willing to get up early or stay up late for?
- A habit you've kept for years, even when people told you to drop it?
- Whose life around you have you envied? Which part?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- Looking back at your big decisions, what mattered most at the time?
- The last time you said no, what were you protecting?
- Stability, freedom, achievement, being loved, being respected, growth: how would you rank them? Can you give an example for the top one?
- Between "want to" and "should", which side do you usually land on?
- What are your lines? Has anyone crossed one?
- What would you give up some comfort for?
- "Earn more but less free" or "earn less but freer": which would you pick, and why?
- Has your definition of success changed over the past few years?
- Who do you admire most, and who would you least want to become?
- Your most recent hard decision: what reason finally tipped it?
- A choice you made that nobody else believed in? How do you see it now?
- In the schools, companies, or groups you've been part of, which way of doing things could you not accept?
- Someone whose choices you disagree with but still respect? What do you respect?
- If next year you could focus on just one of learning, earning, being with people who matter, or experiencing new things, which would you pick? Is your life set up that way now?
- Something you used to care a lot about that matters less now? What changed it?
- For what would you be willing to disappoint someone?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Which of your values did you choose, and which were handed to you?
- Have you paid a price for a value? Was it worth it?
- If you looked back ten years from now, what would you least want to see yourself giving up, and for what?
- How much does "who I want to become" overlap with "who others want me to become"?
- A time you went against your own principles? What pushed you to? Share only what you want.
- Have you been working hard to prove something? To whom?
- If people remembered only one sentence about your life, what would you want it to be?
- Feel free to skip: is there a "should" you've been carrying that you'd really like to put down?
