# Rest, money and taste (plus travel and entertainment)

Same approach as joy. With spending, only talk about preferences and feelings: never judge whether they spend "right", and no financial advice.

## ☀️ Easy
- How would you love to spend a day off? When did you last actually do that?
- What small thing instantly improves your mood?
- Of your recent purchases, which one felt totally worth the money?
- What styles do you love in spaces, clothes, and design? What do you dislike?
- What have you been rewatching or relistening to lately? (Books, shows, podcasts, accounts, music)
- What kind of shops do you love browsing?
- Any little morning or bedtime ritual?
- What on your phone's home screen or camera roll says something about your taste?
- How did you actually spend your last day off? How did you feel at the end of it?
- The smallest purchase in the last three months that made you happiest?
- Your last outing, even half a day locally: where did you go, and what was the best part?
- What's been sitting in your cart or wishlist the longest? Why haven't you bought it?
- A book, show, game, or album you return to in a certain season or mood?
- Describe the most comfortable piece of clothing you own.
- How do you pass the time while waiting in line or for someone?

## 🌤️ Deeper
- How is the rest that actually recharges you different from the rest you think should relax you?
- What are you happy to spend more on, and what feels like a waste?
- Impulse buyer or researcher? How do you usually feel after buying?
- Describe your most comfortable trip: pace, company, where you stayed, what you ate.
- Plan ahead or decide as you go?
- Lively entertainment or quiet, solo entertainment?
- What do the things you rewatch have in common? (A mood, characters, pacing, a worldview…)
- What's the difference between "happy spending" and "anxious spending" for you?
- A day of holiday that's entirely yours: where do you go and what do you do?
- The last time a break left you more tired: what did you do, and how was it different from a break that worked?
- How do you spend differently when money's tight versus comfortable? Which feels better?
- Something you bought long ago and still love? How is it different from things you regretted?
- On a trip, what do you enjoy most: planning, being on the road, or looking at photos afterwards?
- The last time you did "revenge rest" (scrolling late, shopping sprees), what came before it? What did you actually want?
- Has your taste changed over the years? When was the biggest shift, and why?
- Is there a kind of rest you really want but never manage? What gets in the way?

## 🌙 Late-night
- Is there a kind of spending that makes you feel guilty? Guilty about what?
- Have you ever given up a style you loved to look "better"?
- What quality of life do you feel you "deserve"? Where did that idea come from?
- Do you feel rest has to be earned first, like you can't relax until everything's done? Where does that come from?
- A way of living you admire but feel "isn't for someone like me"?
- The last time you spent money to comfort yourself, what happened that day? Share only what you want.
- If nobody could see, how would your room, your clothes, or your weekends be different?
